# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Yok-AI to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions and experience levels.
- Give and accept useful feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Using the project to help anyone break exam, interview or workplace rules, or record people without the consent the law requires.
- Any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, code, issues or pull requests that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All reports are kept confidential.
